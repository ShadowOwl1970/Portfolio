Featured cover concept

Story translated into visual identity.

This collection lets me connect the written world of The Other Side with a visual identity through character illustration, symbolic details, composition, and publication-focused artwork.

The featured cover began as a school project inspired by Alphonse Mucha, and I plan to continue refining it into the final cover for the novel.

My approach

Designing the novel as a visual world.

01

Story

The narrative, themes, and characters establish the foundation for each illustration.

02

Symbolism

Decorative elements, color, and recurring motifs reinforce themes and character identity.

03

Publication

Typography, framing, and hierarchy are considered alongside the illustration when the work is intended for a book cover.

04

Refinement

Personal and academic pieces can continue evolving as the novel and its visual direction develop.
