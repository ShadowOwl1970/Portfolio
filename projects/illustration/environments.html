Featured environment

Building a place through atmosphere.

Environment illustration gives me room to build mood through color, lighting, shape, and scale. I focus on the feeling of a place as much as the individual objects within it.

Simplifying forms helps me control the composition and guide attention while still creating a scene that feels immersive and complete.

My approach

Building atmosphere with intention.

01

Composition

Large shapes and focal points establish how the viewer moves through the scene.

02

Palette

Color relationships establish mood, time of day, weather, and visual hierarchy.

03

Depth

Foreground, middle ground, background, scale, and overlap help create a convincing sense of space.

04

Atmosphere

Lighting, clouds, weather, and environmental effects give each scene its emotional tone.

Environment illustration

Creating places that feel lived in.

Even a simplified environment can suggest a much larger world when composition, atmosphere, and scale work together.

I use shape and color to establish the scene first, then add enough detail to support the setting without overwhelming the composition.

This collection can continue growing as I explore new locations, lighting conditions, architectural spaces, and natural environments.
