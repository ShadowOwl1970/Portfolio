Featured illustration

A moment can tell a larger story.

Narrative illustration lets me combine character design, composition, expression, and atmosphere to create a moment that feels connected to a larger story.

I think about what happened before the image, what is happening within it, and what the viewer might imagine happens afterward.

My approach

Building a scene around a story.

01

Moment

I begin with the story beat or emotional moment the illustration needs to communicate.

02

Characters

Pose, expression, proximity, and interaction help establish relationships and emotion.

03

Composition

Framing, scale, focal points, and negative space guide attention through the scene.

04

Mood

Color, lighting, environment, and effects reinforce the emotional tone of the story moment.

Visual storytelling

Illustrating moments that feel alive.

A single illustration can suggest relationships, conflict, history, and emotion without explaining every part of the story.

I use character acting and composition to establish the narrative first, then support it with environment, color, lighting, and detail.

This collection can continue growing as I explore new characters, fantasy settings, relationships, and story moments.
