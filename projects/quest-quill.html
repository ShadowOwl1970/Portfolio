Quest Quill.

Quest Quill is a fantasy-inspired graphic design project built around visual storytelling, strong thematic identity, and a flexible system for presenting imaginative content.

Overview

Building a world through design.

Quest Quill was designed around the idea that visual identity can help establish atmosphere before a viewer ever reads the full story.

The project combines graphic design and illustration to create a fantasy-inspired system with enough personality to feel distinctive while still remaining practical across multiple applications.

The goal was to create an identity that feels imaginative, cohesive, and flexible enough to support a wider visual world.

The challenge

Fantasy without visual chaos.

Fantasy-inspired design can easily become overly decorative or difficult to read.

The challenge was to create a strong thematic atmosphere without allowing ornamental elements to overwhelm the actual information.

The visual system needed to balance character, readability, consistency, and flexibility.

Design goals

A system made for storytelling.

01

Atmosphere

Establish a recognizable fantasy tone through visual language and supporting imagery.

02

Clarity

Keep text and information easy to understand despite the more expressive visual direction.

03

Consistency

Create repeated visual patterns that make different materials feel part of the same identity.

04

Flexibility

Allow the identity to grow into new assets without losing its overall personality.

Visual direction

Setting the tone.

The visual direction draws from fantasy imagery, handcrafted details, and storybook-inspired design.

Decorative elements establish the mood, while cleaner structural choices create enough contrast to keep the identity usable.

This balance helps the project feel thematic without becoming difficult to navigate visually.

Identity

Creating a recognizable mark.

The identity needed a visual anchor that could immediately communicate the tone of Quest Quill.

Logo and mark development focused on creating something distinct enough to feel memorable while still supporting the larger visual system.

The final direction works alongside supporting graphics rather than trying to carry the entire identity alone.

Reflection

Storytelling can be visual too.

Quest Quill helped strengthen the connection between my graphic design and illustration work.

The project reinforced how atmosphere can be created through consistent visual choices rather than relying on one decorative element.

It also helped me think more intentionally about creating identity systems that can support both practical communication and imaginative storytelling.
