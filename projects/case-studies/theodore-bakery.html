Theodore Bakery.

Theodore Bakery is a visual identity and packaging project centered around creating a warm, approachable bakery brand with a recognizable personality across customer touchpoints.

Overview

A brand that feels fresh from the oven.

Theodore Bakery was approached as more than a logo. The identity needed to create a complete visual experience around the bakery and its products.

From packaging to promotional materials, each touchpoint needed to feel connected to the same personality.

The project focused on building a flexible identity that feels inviting, memorable, and appropriate for a customer-facing food brand.

The challenge

Familiar, but still memorable.

Bakery branding often relies on familiar ideas of warmth, comfort, craft, and tradition.

The challenge was to communicate those qualities without creating an identity that felt interchangeable with other bakery brands.

The final system needed enough personality to stand apart while remaining approachable to a broad audience.

Visual direction

Finding the balance between craft and comfort.

The visual direction was built around creating an inviting identity with a handcrafted personality.

Typography, color, illustration, and supporting graphics work together to establish a recognizable tone without overwhelming the products themselves.

This gives the bakery enough personality to feel distinctive while maintaining the simplicity needed for everyday use.

Logo development

Giving Theodore a face.

The logo serves as the primary point of recognition throughout the Theodore Bakery identity.

Early concepts explored different ways to communicate warmth and personality while maintaining enough simplicity for practical applications.

Refinement focused on creating a mark that could remain recognizable on everything from larger signage to smaller packaging elements.

Brand system

The ingredients behind the identity.

Typography and color expand the personality established by the logo and create consistency across the wider brand.

The system was designed to provide enough variation for different applications while maintaining a clear connection between them.

Supporting graphics and layout treatments complete the visual language and make it easier to extend the brand into future materials.

Packaging

Branding customers can take with them.

Packaging is one of the most visible extensions of a bakery's identity.

Boxes, bags, labels, cups, and other customer-facing materials provide opportunities to reinforce the brand after a purchase leaves the bakery.

The packaging system carries the same visual language as the larger identity while remaining practical for everyday use.

Final result

Reflection

Designing beyond the logo.

Theodore Bakery reinforced how much of a brand experience happens outside of the primary logo.

Packaging, typography, color, graphics, and environmental details all contribute to how customers recognize and remember a business.

The project strengthened my approach to building visual identities that remain cohesive across both practical and expressive applications.
