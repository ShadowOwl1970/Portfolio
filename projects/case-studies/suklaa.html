Suklaa Chocolatiera.

Suklaa Chocolatiera is a Finnish-inspired chocolate brand created for the U.S. market. The project combines Nordic craft, premium packaging, visual storytelling, and a digital experience designed to make the brand feel imaginative, warm, and memorable.

Role

Brand Designer
Web Designer

Project Type

Brand Identity · Packaging · Website

Focus

Visual Identity · Product Packaging · Digital Experience

Tools

Illustrator · Photoshop · Figma · Webflow

Overview

Finnish heritage, reimagined.

Suklaa was created to introduce Finnish chocolate craft to a U.S. audience without losing the warmth, artistry, and tradition behind the brand.

The identity draws from Nordic craftsmanship, nature, generational storytelling, and the transformative feeling of discovering a thoughtfully made chocolate.

The result is a premium brand system that can move naturally between packaging, merchandise, physical environments, and a digital storefront.

The challenge

Tradition without feeling dated.

Suklaa needed to communicate heritage and craftsmanship while still feeling contemporary enough for a modern U.S. market.

The identity also needed enough flexibility to support multiple flavors, packaging variations, merchandise, digital experiences, and future brand growth without losing a recognizable visual core.

Brand strategy

Craft meets transformation.

Suklaa combines the Creator and Magician archetypes.

The Creator supports craftsmanship, originality, and attention to detail. The Magician introduces transformation, discovery, and a sense of wonder.

Together, they support four core values: transformation, craftsmanship, sustainability, and imagination.

Visual identity

Rich, Nordic, and magical.

The visual system pairs dark, atmospheric tones with warm gold and expressive celestial details.

Velvet Cocoa and Starfall Navy establish depth and sophistication, while Twilight Indigo adds a more imaginative edge. Honeyed Gold brings warmth and premium contrast.

Packaging system

One identity, many flavors.

Each chocolate bar uses the same core composition while color, border treatments, and flavor naming create enough variation for the line to feel collectible without losing brand recognition.

Brand applications

Built beyond the wrapper.

The identity was designed to remain recognizable across retail, merchandise, packaging, and everyday customer touchpoints.

Applying the monogram to signage and merchandise helped test whether the brand could retain its premium personality outside the chocolate packaging itself.

Digital experience

Translating the brand to the screen.

The website extends Suklaa's visual language into a digital experience focused on storytelling, products, community, and brand discovery.

The site was structured around five primary destinations: Home, About, Product Showcase, Community, and Contact.

Information architecture

A simple path through the brand.

The sitemap keeps the experience intentionally focused, giving users direct access to the brand story, products, community content, and contact information.

Outcome

One brand, many touchpoints.

Suklaa became a complete identity system rather than a single logo or website.

Packaging, merchandise, physical applications, and the digital experience all use the same visual language while adapting to the needs of each format.

The project strengthened my ability to build a brand from strategy through execution and carry that identity into a cohesive digital experience.
