Featured design

Character begins with personality.

My character work begins with more than appearance. I think about who the character is, what world they belong to, and what their clothing, silhouette, accessories, expression, and color choices communicate before they ever speak.

Some designs begin with a story while others begin with a visual idea, but each one becomes an opportunity to explore personality through design.

My approach

Building characters from the details.

01

Silhouette

Shape, proportions, hair, wings, horns, clothing, and other major forms establish the character before smaller details are added.

02

Personality

Expressions, poses, gestures, and visual details help communicate who the character is.

03

Worldbuilding

Clothing, weapons, companions, and fantasy elements connect the character to the world they inhabit.

04

Color

Color palettes help establish mood, personality, visual hierarchy, and recognition.

Character design gives me a way to combine illustration, storytelling, and visual problem solving.

As my work continues to develop, this collection will grow alongside the characters and worlds I create.
