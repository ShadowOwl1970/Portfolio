Featured PNGTuber

A streaming persona with personality.

PNGTuber design lets me combine character illustration with expressive avatar design for streamers and online creators. Each design needs to read clearly at small sizes while still feeling personal and recognizable.

Many of the characters happen to be animal-inspired, but the goal is always to create a memorable persona that feels natural to the creator using it.

My approach

Designing avatars for creators.

01

Readability

Strong silhouettes and clear facial features help the avatar remain readable on stream and at smaller sizes.

02

Expressions

Expression sets give creators multiple emotional states they can use while speaking or reacting.

03

Identity

Color, accessories, species traits, clothing, and recurring details make each avatar feel specific to its creator.

04

Streaming Use

The final artwork is designed to function as a practical streaming asset while still standing on its own as character art.
