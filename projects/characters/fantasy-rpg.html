Featured design

Design that feels part of a world.

Fantasy character design gives me room to combine illustration with worldbuilding. I think about where a character comes from, what role they fill, what they carry, and how their clothing and physical features communicate their history.

Details like armor, horns, wings, weapons, companions, and layered clothing become more than decoration when they help explain the character before their story is told.

My approach

Designing beyond the character.

01

Role

The character's class, occupation, personality, and story role establish the direction of the design.

02

World

Clothing, materials, culture, environment, and fantasy elements connect the character to their setting.

03

Equipment

Weapons, armor, tools, magical objects, and companions help communicate how the character moves through the world.

04

Identity

Silhouette, color, expression, and recurring motifs give each character a recognizable visual identity.

Fantasy design

A character should feel like they belong.

My favorite fantasy designs are the ones where the character and the world feel inseparable.

Costume, equipment, creature traits, and color can all suggest culture, profession, environment, and history. Those choices help turn a visual concept into someone who feels like they could exist beyond a single illustration.

This collection will continue to grow as I create new characters for tabletop games, original worlds, and fantasy-focused projects.
