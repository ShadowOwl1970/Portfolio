Featured illustration

Recognizable, even simplified.

For this series, I simplify each agent while preserving the visual traits that make them recognizable. Silhouette, expression, signature colors, hairstyles, clothing, and props do most of the work.

The chibi format lets me exaggerate personality and expression while practicing consistency across a larger character collection.

My approach

Simplifying without losing identity.

Fan art series

Building a series that feels consistent.

A large character series needs enough consistency to feel connected while still allowing every agent to remain distinct.

Using the same chibi proportions and rendering approach creates a shared visual system, while each agent's palette, silhouette, and signature details preserve their individual identity.

The series can continue growing as I add more agents while keeping the same visual language across the collection.
